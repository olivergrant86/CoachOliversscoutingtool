Grant's Basic Instructions:
Top right add an opponent/season. After you hit add make sure that is selected and then you can start importing. When you import take the gamechanger play by play and paste it into the game log box. You do not have to put the team names in there but I would highly suggest putting the home and visitor team names in the box just so their are no mismatched names if someone spelled something wrong in their Gamechanger. After this just click the Parse and Add to season button and let the tool do its magic.

Two players with the same short name in this game? (e.g. brothers "K Garrett")

GameChanger writes every player as first-initial + last name, so two players who share both come through as one identical name in the play log — this app can't tell them apart from that text alone. If that's happening, look up each one's spot in the batting order (1st, 2nd, 3rd...) from the box score, and enter it below — one line per player, format: Short Name, Order Slot, Full Name to Use
